• BIOGRAPHY • THE FULL STORY

How I Started & Why I Keep Building

Ever since I was a kid, I was completely obsessed with technology. While others just used computers or phones, I wanted to know how they worked inside. I spent endless hours tinkering with laptops, PCs, and mobile software. I still remember rooting my own phones multiple times just to install custom ROMs and see what I could unlock.

Right after passing my SSC exams in Class 10, I officially took my first step into coding. I moved over to Linux, spent countless hours working in the terminal breaking and fixing things and started building my own small SaaS projects on GitHub. That early curiosity gave me the technical mindset I use today.

Around late 2020, my curiosity naturally pulled me into the world of crypto and Web3. But honestly, my entry was far from smooth. I had no mentor and no proper roadmap. Like most beginners, I made mistakes lost money, fell for bad projects, and faced days where nothing went right. It was frustrating, but quitting was never an option. Instead of walking away, I decided to treat every loss as a lesson and began studying market mechanics deeply.

To understand how online communities function, I started working as a moderator for various Web3 groups. I spent long hours helping users, managing discussions, and learning community dynamics. At the same time, I was trading Forex and crypto to get a real feel for market movements and risk management.

No forms. No hoops. Just this.
